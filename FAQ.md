# NxNow – FAQ & Tips

Everything you need to know to get the best possible gaming experience with NxNow on your Nintendo Switch.

## 1. How do I log in to Steam, EA, or Battle.net?

When launching a game for the first time through Steam, EA (Electronic Arts), or Battle.net, you may need to enter your username and password.

NxNow provides two convenient ways to do this:

**Option 1 – Nintendo Switch Virtual Keyboard**

- Press **ZR + R3** to open the virtual keyboard.
- Enter your login credentials directly on your Nintendo Switch.

**Option 2 – Mouse & Keyboard Mode**

- Press **L3 + R3** to switch between Controller Mode and Mouse & Keyboard Mode.
- Use the **left stick** to move the mouse cursor.
- Press **ZR** for a left mouse click.
- Press **ZL** for a right mouse click.
- Select the login fields and use the virtual keyboard to enter your credentials.

Once you're logged in, simply press **L3 + R3** again to return to Controller Mode.

## 2. Can I use the keyboard to chat while gaming?

Absolutely!

While playing, you can open the Nintendo Switch virtual keyboard at any time by pressing **ZR + R3**.

This allows you to communicate with your teammates, participate in in-game chats, and type messages without leaving your gaming session.

## 3. Does NxNow support physical keyboards and mice?

Yes! NxNow supports USB keyboards and mice connected directly to your Nintendo Switch.

You can connect them using:

- A compatible **USB-C hub** in handheld mode.
- The **Nintendo Switch Dock** and its standard USB ports when playing on your TV.

Connected keyboards and mice are recognized automatically, allowing you to enjoy a more traditional PC gaming experience.

This is particularly useful for games such as World of Warcraft and Counter-Strike.

## 4. What should I do if my streaming connection is unstable?

If your internet connection is slow, unreliable, or inconsistent, you can adjust your streaming settings at any time.

For a more stable experience, try:

- **Frame Rate:** 30 FPS
- **Maximum Bitrate:** 8 Mbps

Reducing the frame rate and bitrate lowers bandwidth requirements and can help prevent freezing, stuttering, and connection interruptions.

You can increase the streaming quality again whenever your connection allows it. On a good connection, **20 Mbps** is the recommended maximum bitrate.

## 5. Does NxNow support 60 FPS in World of Warcraft and Counter-Strike?

Yes!

NxNow's streaming module now supports **up to 60 FPS** in games such as:

- **World of Warcraft (WoW)**
- **Counter-Strike (CS)**

This provides a much smoother and more responsive gaming experience compared to 30 FPS.

Actual performance depends on your network connection, streaming settings, and server performance.

## 6. Why is the Steam Big Picture intro video stuttering?

You might notice that the Steam Big Picture startup animation occasionally appears choppy or stutters when launching a Steam game.

**Don't worry — this is not an NxNow issue.**

This behavior originates from the NVIDIA streaming environment rather than NxNow itself.

Once the introduction finishes and your game starts, streaming should return to normal.

There is no need to restart NxNow just because the introduction appears choppy.

## 7. Does NxNow support audio and microphone streaming?

**Game audio: yes.** NxNow streams your game's sound to the Nintendo Switch, with dedicated handling to keep playback smooth alongside the video and controls.

**Microphone: not yet.** Voice chat through the Switch microphone is not supported at the moment. It is on the roadmap for a future update.

## 8. Can I switch between Controller Mode and Mouse & Keyboard Mode while playing?

Yes!

Simply press **L3 + R3** at any time to toggle between the two input modes.

**Controller Mode:** Play using the Nintendo Switch controls as a standard gamepad.

**Mouse & Keyboard Mode:** Use the left stick to control the mouse cursor, ZR for left click, and ZL for right click.

You can switch between these modes without restarting your game or streaming session.
